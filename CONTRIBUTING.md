# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in jeuchre!

## Ground rules

- **Issues are open to everyone** — rules questions, bug reports, ideas, variant proposals.
- **Small PRs are welcome** (typos, bug fixes, docs). For anything bigger, **open an issue
  first** so we can agree on direction — the roadmap is owner-driven (see PLAN.md).
- The canonical jeuchre rules change only by decision of the rules steward (Julian). Variants
  are encouraged — under CC BY-SA 4.0 you're free to publish your own, with attribution.
- Be kind: [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Development setup

See the Developing section of [README.md](README.md). This repo is heavily developed with AI
coding agents; [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) documents the conventions both humans and agents follow.

## Licensing of contributions

Code contributions are accepted under MIT; content contributions (rules text, prose, images)
under CC BY-SA 4.0.
